Cards have 18px corners, keys 12px. Main content stays 100px from canvas edges. Depth comes from a restrained ink shadow and deliberate overlap of an overlay on the fictional message window.

Background: editorial rules and a slowly travelling progress bar. Silence gives text time to be read. Avoid gradients, neon, fake metrics and simulated real customer data.
